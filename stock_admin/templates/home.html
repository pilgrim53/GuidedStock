Designed for simplicity

GuidedStock's simplified dashboard lets you quickly identify stocks to buy, sell, or hold--according to your goals.

  • Simple signals with green (buy), yellow (hold), and red (sell) indicators right on the ticker.
  • Sorted by proximity to your buy/sell targets, so stocks ready to sell are at the top, and those within your purchase price at the bottom.
  • Your total investment and current profit are shown prominently so you don't lose track.

Fast and easy editing

Clicking on any of the stocks in your watchlist will open the drawer with more information about the stock. From here you're able to make updates quickly and easily.

  • Update your buy/sell targets.
  • Enter purchased stock and price.
  • Delete stocks you're no longer following.
